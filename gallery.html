Download Video File
Full Speech Transcript:

"Greetings, my name is Ndyamuhaki Samuel, a proud undergraduate student pursuing a Bachelor of Business Computing at Bugema University."

"My journey into this course was sparked by a fundamental observation in our local commercial ecosystem: modern businesses possess ambitious visions, but they consistently lose momentum due to disorganized spreadsheets, unintegrated databases, and lack of real-time operational insights. I realized that writing code in isolation isn't enough; businesses need technologists who deeply comprehend accounting cycles, supply chain bottlenecks, and customer value streams."

"Through Bugema University's comprehensive curriculum, I am mastering relational database administration with SQL, modern web architectures, enterprise resource planning, and data analytics. My ultimate motivation is to build resilient systems that empower enterprises in East Africa and globally to scale with confidence, accuracy, and operational integrity. Thank you."
